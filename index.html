Dear Beloved Guests,

We can’t wait to celebrate this beautiful day with you! To help everything flow smoothly, we’d love your kind understanding with a few gentle reminders.

Ceremony Reminders

• Don’t be late! We’d hate for you to miss the “I do’s” come early, grab your seat, and soak up the moment with us.
• Phones down, hearts open. Our ceremony is unplugged let’s keep eyes on love, not screens. (Don’t worry, the pros will capture it all!)
• Shhh… love is in the air. Please help us keep the ceremony calm and sacred save the cheers and big hugs for after the kiss.
• Little ones. As much as we love kids, the ceremony may not be the best place for tiny adventurers, but if they must join, kindly keep a loving eye on them.

Reception Reminders

• Eat, drink & be merry! After the vows, it’s time to feast, laugh, and dance the night away.
• Bring your sparkle. Dress to impress and add a touch of gold if you like you’ll make the night shine brighter.
• Go with the flow. Our coordinators will guide the seating, meals, and program so all you have to do is enjoy.
• Snap & share! This is your time to take all the selfies, dance shots, and candid moments. Don’t forget to tag them with #MichaelAnganSiElcia so we can relive the fun.
• Cheers responsibly. Let’s toast, celebrate, and dance safely because the best memories are made when we remember them tomorrow.

Gift Reminder

• Your presence is truly the best gift we could ask for. But if you wish to bless us more, a monetary gift would be greatly appreciated as we start our journey together. Thank you for sharing your love and generosity with us!

Where to stay after the event?

• Our venue is just a short drive from Tagaytay! Feel free to book a stay nearby so you can continue exploring and enjoying the area even after our special day.

More than anything, we just want you to relax, have fun, and share this special day with us your presence truly means a lot.

Sincerely,
Michael & Elcia
